Nomentra Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026.

Who we are

Nomentra is provided by Ossian House Holding Ltd.

You can contact us at support@nomentra.app.

What Nomentra uses

Nomentra turns location information into a daily travel history that you can review and correct.

Photos and videos

With your permission, the scan reads photo and video capture dates and embedded location metadata from your Photos library.

The iOS photo permission message says: “Nomentra scans photo and video dates and location metadata to build your travel history. Your media stays in your photo library.”

Hidden-album items are excluded from the scan by default.

The current phone copy also reads local image previews, assesses them on the device to choose travel photos, and displays those photos inside the app.

Those image requests do not allow network access, and this flow does not upload your media.

Device location

With your permission, Nomentra receives visits and significant location changes from iOS.

The iOS foreground location message says: “Nomentra uses your location while the app is open to record place evidence for your travel history. Exact coordinates are not stored.”

The iOS background location message says: “Nomentra uses significant location changes and visits in the background to keep your travel history up to date. Exact coordinates are not stored.”

A location event begins as a coordinate held for processing.

Nomentra keeps the event’s date and time, source, named place, time zone, confidence, and location-quality details, but it does not store the coordinate or a map trail.

The saved App Store privacy answer lists Precise Location for app functionality, not linked to identity, and not used to track you across apps or websites.

Your entries and choices

Nomentra stores your confirmations, corrections, home-country choices, and app settings on the device.

The app has no sign-in or user account.

What leaves the device

Nomentra does not send your stored travel history, confirmations, settings, photos, or videos to Ossian House or to advertising services.

For a place lookup, Nomentra sends the coordinate for that one event to Apple’s map service and receives a place name; it does not send the photo or your travel history with that request.

Apple also operates the App Store through which Nomentra and any optional app-owned content are delivered.

If you email support, you choose the message and send it through your mail provider.

Do not include your travel history, place names, travel dates, photos, photo identifiers, or coordinates in a support message.

The current phone copy does not collect diagnostics and contains no third-party analytics service.

Storage and retention

Nomentra’s live data store is on the device and has iCloud storage turned off.

Your original photos and videos remain in your Photos library.

Nomentra keeps its evidence, travel history, corrections, and record of changes until you choose to remove them; it does not silently expire them.

Your controls

You can change Nomentra’s Photos and Location permissions in iOS Settings.

You can export a copy of your Nomentra record to a file you choose.

You can choose Delete All Data inside Nomentra to remove its evidence, places, travel days, suggestions, corrections, confirmations, and record of changes from the device.

Children

Nomentra is not directed at children under 13.

Nomentra does not create accounts for children or adults.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we will give the revised policy a new date and publish it at https://nomentra.app/privacy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@nomentra.app.
