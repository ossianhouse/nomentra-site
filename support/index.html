Nomentra Support

Nomentra turns location information into a clear daily history of where you have been.

It brings together photo and device-location evidence, then lets you review and correct the result.

It also helps you explore past travel and review your record without replacing advice from a suitably qualified professional.

Get help

Email support@nomentra.app.

We aim to reply within five business days.

What to include

Please include the Nomentra app version, your iOS version, your device model, what happened, and the steps that led to the problem.

Do not send your travel history, country or place names, travel dates, photos, photo identifiers, or coordinates.
