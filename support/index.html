Reporting a problem

These details help us find the cause:

  • App version and build. In Nomentra, open Settings and scroll to the bottom. It looks like “1.0 (102)”.
  • iOS version and iPhone model. In iPhone Settings, open General, then About.
  • What happened. What you did, what you expected, and what you saw instead.

Keep your record private

You don’t need to send your travel history, your photos or screenshots of your record. If one would help, we will ask first, and it is always your choice.

We use your email only to reply and to fix the problem. See the Privacy Policy.
