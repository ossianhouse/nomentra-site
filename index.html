Private beta · iPhone

Know where you’ve been.

Nomentra builds a history of your travels from your photos and permitted iPhone location. Then you review and correct it.

  • Understand your travels

    See where you were, day by day: the countries you visited, your trips, and the days you spent in each.

  • Keep your history updated

    New photos and permitted location updates add to your record as you go. It isn’t a continuous GPS trace, so some days may need your input.

  • Stay in control

    Your record is stored on your iPhone, with no account. Correct it, export a copy or delete it whenever you choose.
