Nomentra turns the photos already on your iPhone into the story of everywhere you have been. Then it keeps score: the countries you have set foot in, the capitals and cities you have collected, your longest trip, your travel streak, and the days that matter for tax and visa rules.

  • Collect the world

    Every country you have ever visited, found in your own photos. Capitals, towns and cities, US states, world heritage sites: each one a badge, each one with your photo on it. Watch the map fill in. See how far your longest trip took you, and how many days in a row you have been on the road.

  • Relive every trip

    A calendar of every day with the flag of where you were. A page for every country and every trip, with your pictures. Your first visit, your longest stay, your latest day. Nothing to type: your history builds itself, and your iPhone’s location keeps it going.

  • Know where you stand

    Tax residence, visas, settlement, citizenship: most of them come down to days. Nomentra counts yours for the UK, US, Cyprus, South Africa, Schengen, Canada, Ireland, Italy, the UAE, Mexico and US states, with a link to the official rule. One glance shows how many days you have, how many are left, and which days still need a word from you. Need something else? Build your own tracker in a minute and see the result before you save it.

  • Private, on your phone

    No account. Nothing uploaded. No advertising, no tracking, no analytics. Your history lives on your iPhone, and in your own iCloud backup if you switch it on.

Plans

Free: four achievements. Basic: every achievement and two active trackers. Premium: unlimited trackers. Annual subscriptions, with a free trial for eligible new subscribers. Your data and setup are never deleted when a plan changes.
