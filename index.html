Nomentra reads the dates and locations of the photos already on your iPhone and builds your travel history: the countries, trips and days they record. It collects your milestones, lets you tick off the World Heritage sites you have seen, and counts the days that matter for tax and visa rules, with the official rule one tap away. Your photos and videos stay in your photo library.

  • Collect the world

    Find the countries recorded in your photo locations. Collect capitals, towns and cities, US states and World Heritage sites. Watch the map fill in. See how far your longest trip took you, and how many days in a row you have been on the road.

  • Relive your trips

    A calendar of your recorded days and countries. A page for every country and every trip, with your pictures. Your first visit, your longest stay, your latest day. Your history builds from your photo locations. Add your iPhone’s location as you travel, with your permission. Review and fill any gaps.

  • Know where you stand

    Tax residence, visas, settlement, citizenship: Nomentra counts the days for rules in the UK, the US and US states, Schengen, Canada, Ireland and more, with a link to the official rule. One glance shows how many days you have, how many are left, and which days still need a word from you. Need something else? Build your own tracker and see the result before you save it.

  • Private, on your phone

    No account. No advertising, no tracking and no third-party analytics. Your history lives on your iPhone, and in your own iCloud backup if you switch it on.

Plans

Free: four achievements. Basic: every achievement and 2 active day trackers. A tracker you switch off can be switched on again after 30 days. Premium: unlimited day trackers. 3 months free for eligible new subscribers. Renews annually unless cancelled. Your data and setup are never deleted when a plan changes.
