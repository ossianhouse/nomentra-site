For iPhone

Know where you’ve been.

Nomentra builds a history of your travels from your photos and permitted iPhone location. Then you review and correct it.

  • Understand your travels

    See the countries you visited, your trips, and your days in each. Track the day counts that matter to you, such as days in the Schengen area.

  • Keep your history up to date

    Scan new photos and allow iPhone location access to keep your history up to date. No continuous GPS.

  • Stay in control

    Your travel record is stored on your iPhone, with no account. Correct it, export a readable copy or delete it.
